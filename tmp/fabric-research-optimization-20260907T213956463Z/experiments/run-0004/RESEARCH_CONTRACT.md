# Research contract

Produce a decision-grade research-backed operating policy for improving tool-using coding-agent performance WITHOUT changing the model or reasoning effort. R1: What evidence distinguishes context selection, tool interfaces, task contracts, parallelism and recovery interventions, including actual results and methods? R2: Where do more context, more agents, retries or self-critique fail, and what cannot transfer to current GPT-5.6 Terra? R3: Which observed failure should trigger which intervention, with validation and stop conditions? R4: What exact fixed-model paired evaluation could change the recommendation, including accepted-task quality, wall time and failure accounting? Use primary inspected sources and null/regression evidence, retain measured values with conditions, provide a failure-to-intervention matrix and usable evaluation checklist, and a complete source appendix. Research date 2026-09-07. No universal effect sizes or unmeasured performance claims.

## Planned independent streams
1. **context-contracts** — Unique uncertainty: Which controlled evidence separates context selection/window design, task contracts/specification, and tool-interface design, including measured gains, nulls, and mechanisms? Inform R1, R2, R3 and failure-to-intervention matrix rows about ambiguity, irrelevant/overlong context, and tool misuse.
2. **parallel-recovery** — Unique uncertainty: Which controlled or operational evidence distinguishes parallel decomposition/agent count from retries, self-critique, verification and recovery loops? Seek non-monotonicity, correlated failures, budget costs, and stop conditions. Inform R1-R3 matrix rows and R2 transfer limits.
3. **coding-benchmarks** — Unique uncertainty: In tool-using coding-agent benchmarks and systems papers, what fixed-model or paired intervention results exist for scaffolds, tools, context, contracts, parallelism, or recovery? Extract exact harnesses and all invalid comparisons. Inform R1, R2, R4 evaluation design.
4. **counterevidence-evaluation** — Unique uncertainty: Actively seek null/regression evidence and methodological reasons results cannot transfer to current GPT-5.6 Terra. Derive the smallest exact fixed-model paired evaluation, acceptance/failure accounting, and decision-changing thresholds. Inform R2-R4 and checklist.

## Output slots
Executive policy; evidence distinctions; transfer/null limits; failure-to-intervention matrix; fixed-model paired evaluation checklist; source appendix; stream accounting.

## Sufficiency
Each stream returns inspected primary-source evidence or an explicit gap, method, contradictions, confidence, and smallest resolving measurement. Final claims require a direct URL and comparability screening.
