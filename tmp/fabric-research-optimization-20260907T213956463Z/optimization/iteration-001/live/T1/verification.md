## Required bounded corrections

1. **C1:** Replace few-shot CoT PaLM-540B GSM8K **58.1% vs 17.9%, +40.2 pp** with **56.9% vs 17.9%, +39.0 pp**.
2. **C2:** Do not label self-consistency as exactly **40× cost**. It is **40 sampled generations per run** relative to one greedy generation, while token lengths, latency, tool spend, and dollars remain unknown.
3. **C3:** Separate ToT’s **9.0% CoT-SC** and **49% best-of-100 CoT** results. Withhold unverified per-case USD figures. Retain the source’s combined **$106** experiment-total statement only under its stated scope.
4. **C4:** Downgrade the two uninspected `Lost in the Middle` numeric claims to unknown pending direct table/figure extraction.
5. **C5:** Preserve source conditions beside every result. In particular, do not transfer benchmark reasoning accuracy, best-of-six agent success, or simulated WebShop outcomes to production text quality, real transactions, or accepted-task operating cost.
