## Cases and comparisons

T1 and T3 are phase-only adaptations of actual historical questions. Fixed inspected primary corpus isolates extraction/composition/validation from retrieval. All writers get identical INPUT plus same source files and original substantive output requests, not AUDIT or counterpart reports. Two independent baseline compositions per case establish decision-sensitive variation. Independent audit facts and source passages belong only to evaluators.

H1 planned mechanism: source-grounded publication closure. Predicted gain: correct material, source-demonstrable defects and omissions before publication instead of stopping after their detection. Risk: added latency, false reviewer corrections, loss of useful unaffected detail. Minimal phase test reuses exact baseline drafts and frozen evidence, then tests bounded independent check → sole-composer correction → distinct independent recheck. Independent comparison reviewers judge original versus corrected artifacts against decisive sources, separately from the internal publication check. This isolates closure from new retrieval or draft variation. It is not live integration or speed evidence.

H2/H3 remain uncommitted until incumbent losses and H1 results identify worthwhile distinct mechanisms. Retrieval-fidelity fallback is a supported hypothesis from the dropped table cells. Source-bound extraction with explicit primary-conflict context is another hypothesis from historical WebArena/InjecAgent distortions. Do not bundle them into H1.

After a candidate passes phase tests, integrate and exercise its actual changed workflow on fresh live held-out T2 and a distinct actual architecture-tool selection task. Keep topic, frame, tool grants/model/effort and comparison prompts fixed. Live evidence differences are not attributed to composition alone. Independent comparisons use explicit literal artifact mappings. Retain only gains that survive relevant regression checks, then checkpoint and extend regression evidence promptly.

## Acceptance and stopping

Improvement versus incumbent and legacy-quality target are separate. No source/count/length proxy. Material supported details, conclusions, structure and operational usefulness determine quality. Correct identified material defects and independently recheck before accepting a report. Stochastic uncertainty requires repeat/targeted adjudication. Non-improvements count only when conclusive. Each verified promotion resets count. Three consecutive conclusive non-improving hypotheses stop the loop with incumbent preserved.
