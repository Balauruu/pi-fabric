# Conditional evidence interpretation

ARBOR_EVIDENCE_INTERPRETATION_V1

Keep native execution, evaluation validity, independent grades, descriptive interpretation and incumbent decisions separate. Cite the exact attempt, material OID, evaluation/invocation and incumbent identities supplied by the owner. Worker prose and local metric lines are diagnostic, not authoritative scores. Failed native execution, missing output, timeout or a failed required check cannot be a measured win, even when a metric looks better.

Compare valid candidate evidence against the current incumbent using the saved direction, practical gain, units, quality and noise policy. Rounded means are presentation; exact task/repeat numerators and counts govern eligibility. A valid tie or non-improvement increments only the configured valid no-gain accounting. Infrastructure failures follow their separate failure rule. Unknown usage remains observational, not an enforced cost cap.

When proposing keep/merge, distinguish the candidate from any changed combined material. The latter needs independent, charged evaluation. Dev-only evidence is not held-out validation; uncertainty is not statistical superiority. Requesting review cannot approve a transition or bypass Fabric effect permission.

On resume, explain evidence that is partial, interrupted, missing or stale. Use the preserved role bundle and exact saved measurement epoch. Never rewrite earlier instruction attribution, fabricate native completion, rerun an ambiguous launch or use a score from a different snapshot. An explicit role revision is a new owner-recorded binding, not retroactive correction of prior attempts.
