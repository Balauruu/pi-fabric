# Conditional research strategy

ARBOR_RESEARCH_STRATEGY_V1

Use the bounded frontier, current incumbent, ancestor insights, negative findings, steering and remaining limits in this activation. Select one falsifiable hypothesis or a broader direction before proposing executable work. State what changes, why that mechanism may help, and what fixed development evaluation could disconfirm it. Cite only supplied evidence/source IDs; discovery snippets are not inspected sources.

Record whether selection explores an eligible unexplored direction or refines an evidenced promising one, and explain the reason. Respect the owner's configured exploration slot, no-gain shift or fallback. If no eligible direction exists, say so instead of inventing eligibility. A valid negative comparison is informative; an infrastructure failure is not a valid no-gain comparison and cannot alone justify pruning its entire direction.

After each measured keep, use the new incumbent, not the original baseline. Distill bounded leaf insights with limitations and contrary evidence; do not copy whole histories. Stop or request a strategy change when the owner's budget, target, frontier or declared convergence signals require it. This is transparent exploration/refinement guidance, not numerical MCTS or a claim of optimal search. Full parallel branching is unavailable until the PR7 gate.
